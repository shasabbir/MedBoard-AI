# Overview
Anaemia is a condition in which red blood cell number or haemoglobin concentration is lower than expected. Common symptoms can include tiredness, dizziness, and shortness of breath, but evaluation requires clinical context and measurement.

# Iron deficiency pattern
Iron deficiency is a common cause of anaemia. Haemoglobin, red-cell indices such as mean corpuscular volume, and markers of iron stores such as ferritin can contribute to assessment. Anaemia is not synonymous with iron deficiency, so alternative causes and sources of blood loss require clinician review.
