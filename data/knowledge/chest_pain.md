# Symptoms
Chest discomfort may include pressure, tightness, or discomfort outside the chest. Shortness of breath and fatigue may accompany cardiac presentations, although many chest-pain episodes have non-cardiac causes.

# Structured evaluation
The guideline emphasizes timely clinical evaluation and structured risk assessment for acute or stable chest pain. Evidence-based diagnostic pathways support decisions about testing and urgency; symptoms alone do not establish a diagnosis.
