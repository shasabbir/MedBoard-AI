# Symptoms
Pneumonia is a lung infection that may be caused by bacteria, viruses, or less commonly other organisms. Reported symptoms can include cough, fever or chills, fatigue, shortness of breath, chest pain with breathing or coughing, and confusion.

# Clinical context
Symptoms overlap with other respiratory illnesses, and clinicians may not always identify the causative organism. Exposure setting, severity, underlying conditions, and examination help guide professional evaluation.
