# Warning features
Sudden weakness, facial changes, speech disturbance, dizziness, loss of balance, or vision loss may be signs of stroke. Stroke is a medical emergency and suspected cases require urgent professional assessment.

# Time and assessment
The time symptoms began or the person was last known well is important to clinical decision-making. Diagnosis distinguishes interruption of blood flow from bleeding and requires appropriate professional examination and investigation.
